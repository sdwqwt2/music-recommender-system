\subsection{Catalog Health and Beyond-Accuracy Diversity}
Evaluating recommendation exposure across all 98,504 tracks refutes the hypothesis that RL alignment narrows recommendations into a small cluster of hit songs~\cite{petrov2024aligning} (Figure~\ref{fig:catalog_and_rollouts}A):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Expanded Catalog Coverage:} Base covers 30.16\% (29,709 items), whereas DPO reaches \textbf{30.36\% (29,906 items)}, introducing users to \textbf{+197 additional unique tracks} from the long tail.
    \item \textbf{Increased Shannon Entropy:} From 13.754 to \textbf{13.786 bits}, indicating a more uniform distribution of recommendations.
    \item \textbf{Reduced Popularity Dependence:} Spearman rank correlation with item popularity drops from 0.5481 to \textbf{0.4748} (\textbf{$-13.4\%$ relative reduction}), demonstrating the model's ability to uncover high-retention niche items.
\end{enumerate}
